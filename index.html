Dzemal Crnkic

Nasheed Artist & Composer · جمال

Dzemal Crnkic is a Nasheed artist and composer whose voice carries the spiritual weight of a deep faith and the cultural richness of the Bosnian Muslim tradition. Rooted in Islamic values, his music bridges generations — bringing the timeless beauty of devotional song to contemporary audiences.

With a career spanning music production, live performance, and community events, Dzemal has become a beloved voice in the Islamic music community. His work reflects his commitment to faith, family, and the preservation of spiritual art through sound.

His music is distributed through AnimSay, LLC — a platform dedicated to supporting independent artists and providing professional event production through AnimSay Event Technologies.
